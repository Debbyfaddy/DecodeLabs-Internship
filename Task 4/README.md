# Task 4: Sales Dashboard Insights & Recommendations

This project focuses on creating an interactive Excel dashboard to visualize sales and operational data. This dashboard provides a structured view of sales performance, customer behavior, and revenue trends. It highlights key metrics across products, months, payment methods, and referral sources, offering actionable insights for decision‑making.

# Key Insights
A total of 1,200 orders were processed during the analysis period.
Chairs were identified as the top-selling product.
The average selling price of items was $1,053.97.
Product revenue varied significantly, with Chairs, Printers, and Laptops generating the highest revenue.
Online payment was the most preferred payment method among customers.
Instagram was the leading referral source, contributing the highest number of customer visits.
Cancelled and returned orders accounted for a significant portion of operational activities, highlighting opportunities for process improvement
