1. Project Overview
This project applies the Input-Process-Output (IPO) Framework to conduct an extensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a transactional e-commerce dataset containing 1,200 unique records. The objective was to clean the raw evidence, run comprehensive descriptive statistics, evaluate transaction anomalies, and isolate key performance trends across product lines, customer payment preferences, and marketing referral pathways.

2. Tools Used
Microsoft Excel: Utilized as the primary data engine for deep data cleaning, sorting, structured pivot table aggregations, key performance indicator (KPI) tracking, and baseline dashboard visualization.

3. Input-Process-Output (IPO) Framework
Inputs
Raw transactional e-commerce dataset containing 1,200 rows.

Features including Product categories, Quantities, Unit Prices, Total Prices, Payment Methods, and Referral Sources.

Processes
Data Cleaning: Standardized text casing, verified numerical consistency across price columns, and structured the data into relational layouts.

Descriptive Analytics: Calculated central tendency metrics including the mean and median for sales volume and transaction values.

Segmentation: Aggregated metrics across payment types, marketing channels, and inventory categories to isolate top-performing business drivers.

Outputs
Cleaned and normalized transactional master data sheet.

Structured summary matrix tables detailing localized product and channel performance.

Data-backed operational insights and strategic commercial recommendations.

6. Deep-Dive Analytical Findings
A. Product Revenue & Volume Distribution
The data shows a highly competitive distribution among the top hardware lines, with office comfort and printing solutions leading gross revenue:

Chair (Top Revenue Leader): Generated $195,620.11 in total sales and moved the highest volume with 562 units sold.

Printer (Secondary Core Driver): Generated $195,612.61 in total revenue with 542 units sold.

Laptop: Contributed $192,126.56 to gross revenue, moving 535 units.

Tablet: Accounted for $186,568.95 in sales with 497 units sold.

Monitor: Brought in $175,651.41 across 480 units.

Desk: Contributed $167,459.93 in revenue with 508 units sold.

Phone: Generated $151,722.39 with 411 units sold.

B. Customer Acquisition & Referral Networks
Customer acquisition channels are heavily distributed across multiple touchpoints, offering highly diversified paths to conversion:

Instagram (Top Channel): Captured the maximum volume, leading customer traffic with 259 successful orders.

Email Campaigns: Proved highly effective for retention and outreach, securing 250 successful orders.

Google Search: Brought in 241 orders, serving as a vital search intent engine.

Facebook: Contributed 228 orders to the total acquisition funnel.

Direct Referral Systems: Accounted for 222 orders through organic word-of-mouth or partner networks.

C. Customer Payment Preferences
Analyzing checkout trends shows a balanced spread of consumer payment types, indicating that offering multiple merchant pathways directly facilitates high-ticket checkouts:

Online Payment Portals: The top processing channel with 258 transactions.

Cash On Delivery / Manual Cash: Accounted for 246 transactions.

Credit Cards: Used in 234 transactions.

Debit Cards: Accounted for 232 transactions.

Gift Cards: Utilized across 230 transactions.

7. Strategic Business Insights & Recommendations
Maximize High-Margin Bundling: Since Chairs and Printers represent the highest baseline revenue elements, the company should implement premium office hardware bundles (such as matching a Desk, Chair, and Monitor) to scale the Average Order Value beyond the current $1,053.97 baseline.

Capitalize on Visual & Direct Marketing Channels: With Instagram (259 orders) and Email (250 orders) converting at the highest rates, marketing budgets should be optimized to favor high-engagement social media campaigns and targeted email newsletters over flat direct channels.

Maintain Payment Balance: The uniform distribution of payment options (ranging evenly from 230 to 258 orders each) indicates that removing any single payment processing pathway (like Gift Cards or Debit Cards) will result in a direct drop in transaction volumes. Keep all 5 payment paths fully optimized at checkout.
